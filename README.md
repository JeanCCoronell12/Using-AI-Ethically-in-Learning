# Using AI Ethically in Learning 🎓🤖

Collaborative research repository created for our final term presentation. This space centralizes peer-reviewed studies sourced from Google Scholar, literature summaries, citations, and presentation deliverables exploring the ethical use of Artificial Intelligence in education.

## 🎯 Research Objectives
* Analyze key ethical challenges of AI in educational settings (academic integrity, algorithmic bias, data privacy, and over-reliance).
* Identify best practices and frameworks for responsible AI adoption by students and educators.
* Ground our findings in peer-reviewed academic literature.

## 📂 Repository Structure
* **`01_articulos_pdf/`**: Peer-reviewed research papers downloaded from Google Scholar.
* **`02_resumenes_notas/`**: Structured Markdown notes, key takeaways, and APA 7th edition references for each source.
* **`03_presentacion/`**: Presentation script, speaker notes, and exported slide decks.

## 🤝 Team Workflow
1. **Literature Search:** Upload verified PDFs from Google Scholar to `01_articulos_pdf/` using the naming convention: `YEAR_Author_ShortTitle.pdf`.
2. **Synthesis:** Document main findings and citations in `02_resumenes_notas/`.
3. **Version Control:** Pull the latest changes before adding new files and write clear commit messages when pushing updates.
